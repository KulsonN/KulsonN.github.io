Livestream highlights → YouTube Shorts

AutoClipper is a personal desktop application that watches selected Twitch and Kick livestreams, detects the most interesting moments, turns them into vertical short videos with subtitles, and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule.

Who uses it: AutoClipper is a private tool used by a single person – the owner of the YouTube channel @okragle_shoty. It is not offered to the public, it has no user accounts and it is not sold.

What the app does

  • Detects highlights – monitors chat activity of selected livestreams and records short clips of moments when the chat reacts strongly.
  • Edits shorts – converts each clip to a 9:16 vertical video, adds automatic subtitles and a short caption ("hook").
  • Lets the owner decide – every short gets a quality score; the owner reviews and approves shorts in the app or through a private Telegram bot before publishing.
  • Schedules publishing – approved shorts are placed into a daily publishing schedule.
  • Uploads to YouTube – using the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), the app uploads the approved shorts to the owner's own channel, setting the title, description and scheduled publish time.

How it uses YouTube and Google data

  • The owner signs in once with Google (OAuth 2.0) and grants the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • youtube.upload is used only to upload the owner's shorts to the owner's channel. youtube.readonly is used only to show the connected channel's name, avatar and subscriber count inside the app.
  • Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server operated by AutoClipper – there is none.
  • The app does not read, collect or share data of any other YouTube users.
